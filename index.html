ABOUT DR UNATI

A Physician.
A Voice. A Movement.

Dr. Unati Makiwane is a family physician with 20 years of experience with special interest in mental health and chronic disease prevention. She comes from a family of healthcare providers, and is inspired to empower patients to better health.

She is a CanReach Fellow, pursuing Masters in Healthcare Leadership at the University of Warwick. She is the first Female Physician in Canada to host a virtual Men's Health Talk Show which runs bi-weekly where she interacts with a global community of men seeking to lead healthy lives mentally, physically and spiritually.

She is the Founder & CEO of Dr. M Health Corner and Black Birth Revolution — a Consultation Company doing Executive Coaching in Corporate, NGO, Schools and NPOs, black maternal health advocacy through her new book, retreats and workshops.

In June 2021, she worked with the UN Affiliated Agency — The Federation of International Gender and Human Rights. In July 2021, she shared the stage with Dr. Martin Luther King III at the World Diversity Conference, where she took a discourse on her expertise on Men's Mental Health.

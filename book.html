ABOUT THE BOOK

This book is a comprehensive maternal wellness guide designed to support Black women through preconception, pregnancy, postpartum recovery, and early motherhood. Combining medical education, self-advocacy, and culturally informed care, it provides practical tools and evidence-based guidance to help women make confident decisions about their health and their families. It was created to bridge knowledge gaps, encourage early intervention, and promote safer maternal outcomes through education and empowerment.
